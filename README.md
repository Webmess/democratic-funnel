## Team-UL - DIRISA Datathon Qualification (3rd Place 🥉)
## The Democratic Funnel
 Where local democracy leaks before 4 November 2026 — Team UL, DIRISA Student Datathon Challenge 2026

## Background
South Africa heads to the polls for Local Government Elections on 4 November 2026. The Electoral Commission of South Africa (IEC) and related government portals publish extensive data on voter registration, election results, party registration, and ward representation, but this data is spread across multiple systems, in a mix of live dashboards, downloadable files, and geographic atlases, rarely combined into a single coherent picture.

## Question
Which municipalities are most at risk of low turnout on 4 November 2026 — and in each one, is the problem getting people registered, or getting registered people to vote?

## Solution
Combining Census 2022, the IEC registration dashboard, historical results, and geographic data, we decompose each municipality's participation shortfall into a registration leak and a turnout leak, with a dedicated youth lens. We then forecast 2026 turnout using a hierarchical model to provide an interactive dashboard that guides targeted action before election day.
